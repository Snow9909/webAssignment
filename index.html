About the Tavern & Lounge

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by lifelong gamers Marcus Chen and Priya Sharma, The Tabletop Tavern began as a dream to create a welcoming space where strategy, laughter, and community collide. What started as a 6-table café has grown into the city's premier board game lounge and TCG destination.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To foster inclusive gaming communities through exceptional service, curated collections, and memorable experiences.

Vision: To be Southeast Asia's most beloved tabletop gaming destination.

House Rules

Treat all guests with courtesy. No trash talk, gloating, or unsportsmanlike conduct. We're here to have fun together.

Count all pieces before and after play. Report missing components immediately. Replacement fees apply for lost items.

TCG tournament areas are drink-free zones. Snacks are welcome at standard tables only. Use coasters always.

Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Family tables are available in the lounge area.

Celebrate victories — but respect others concentrating. Use private rooms for loud party games.

Official tournament rules apply during events. Judges' decisions are final. Register 15 minutes before start time.

Found damaged games or equipment? Tell staff immediately. Never attempt repairs yourself.
